FreeWing, a computational drug discovery team based in Xi'an, China, is dedicated to technological exploration and practical implementation of AI-assisted small molecule drug discovery.
The mol-lib multi-target small molecule screening library open-sourced in this project is a public deliverable of our self-developed generation technology. It covers six major disease research areas, including anti-aging, autoimmune diseases, metabolic diseases, neurodegenerative diseases, oncology, and rare diseases, aiming to provide high-quality, ready-to-use virtual screening datasets for researchers in computational chemistry and drug discovery worldwide.
All molecules in the library undergo standardized quality control, including general druggability rule filtering, toxic alert substructure screening, and structural diversity deduplication. The output format is compatible with mainstream toolchains such as RDKit, AutoDock Vina, and OpenBabel, and can be directly applied to academic research scenarios including molecular docking, lead compound discovery, and computational methodology validation.
Guided by the principle of open sharing, we hope to promote technical exchanges and industrial progress in drug discovery through open-source datasets.
